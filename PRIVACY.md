# Privacy

AnyClass follows a **local-first** model.

## Current behavior

- Timetable data is stored locally in the browser by default.
- The current product does not upload timetable data to the AnyClass server.
- AnyClass does not require storing your school account password.
- The current import flow does not upload your Cookie, Session, or authentication token.
- The timetable parser reads only the page information required to identify the current timetable and semester.

## When reporting bugs

Do **not** include:

- school account passwords
- Cookies
- Sessions
- authentication tokens
- student numbers
- private timetable data
- screenshots containing sensitive personal information unless they are sanitized first

## Future features

Future features such as calendar subscription or additional sync capabilities may have different data requirements. If those features are introduced, their privacy behavior should be documented separately before release.
